Office network rebuild with 24/7 monitoring

Reconfigured 7 switches and 19 Wi-Fi access points and split the network by area. Then set up Prometheus, Grafana and Alertmanager over the whole office: 41 targets, 21 alert rules, 5 dashboards, alerts straight to Telegram, plus a local-LLM agent that suggests a fix and only acts after approval.

Internet and Wi-Fi disruptions dropped from frequent to 1–2 a week, and problems show up on Telegram before anyone complains.
